TEXTO ORIGINAL SELECIONADO:
Humans cannot drink saline water.
The "simple" hurdle that must be overcome to turn seawater into fresh water is to remove the dissolved salt in seawater.
That may seem as easy as just boiling some seawater in a pan, capturing the steam, and condensing it back into water (distillation).
