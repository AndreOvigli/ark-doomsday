TEXTO ORIGINAL SELECIONADO:
Wash your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, especially during key times when you are most likely to spread germs.
If clean tap water is not available, create a temporary handwashing station by using a large water jug.
If you do not have soap and water, use an alcohol-based hand sanitizer that contains at least 60% alcohol.
Alcohol-based hand sanitizers can quickly reduce the number of germs on hands in some situations. However, sanitizers do not eliminate all types of germs and might not remove harmful chemicals. Hand sanitizers are not as effective when hands are visibly dirty or greasy.
